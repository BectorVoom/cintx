**Phase 19 deviations:**
- The Bessel module exposes **two pairs** (`modified_spherical_bessel_in` for $i_l(x)$ and `modified_spherical_bessel_kn` for $k_l(x)$ — only `i_l` strictly required per RESEARCH §"Type-2 (semi-local)").
