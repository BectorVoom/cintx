<objective>
Build the K-Taylor port foundation that both the scalar-close (19-06) and gradient (19-07) plans build on. This is the missing radial machinery the 19-04 direct-quadrature kernel never had: PySCF's exact table-interpolation modified-spherical-Bessel evaluator (`ECPsph_ine_opt`), the per-shell radial-block evaluator (`ECPrad_part`), and the Type-1 / Type-2 radial assemblers (`type1_rad_part`, `type2_facs_rad`), ported host-first as `*_host()` Rust. This plan delivers the radial primitives; 19-06 wires them into `compute_type1_pair` / `compute_type2_pair`.
