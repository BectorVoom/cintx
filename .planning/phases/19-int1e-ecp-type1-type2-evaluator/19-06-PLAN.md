Land the optional secondary cross-check oracle against libecpint (Shaw & Hill, JCP 147 074108, 2017, MIT). This plan is independent of CI — failures do not block — and exists to provide a second independent reference for the ECP byte-identity work landed in Plans 04 and 05.
