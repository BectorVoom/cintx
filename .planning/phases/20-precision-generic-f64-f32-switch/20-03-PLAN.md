<objective>
Wave 1 (shared math leaves, group B): genericize `rys.rs` — the single largest f64 file in the project (1,140 f64-lines, 14 `#[cube]` functions: Rys roots/weights, Clenshaw and Horner polynomial evaluators) — and the cart-to-sph transform `c2s.rs`. Isolated in its own plan because of its size (genericizing it alongside other files would exceed the ~50% context budget).

Purpose: Rys quadrature is the numerical core of every 2e/2c2e/3c1e/3c2e/4c1e/f12 kernel. Its f64 monomorphization must stay byte-identical (the Phase 10 oracle gates depend on it); its f32 monomorphization is where most single-precision error accumulates (informing the Wave 5 tolerance floors). `c2s.rs` shares the rational-coefficient-table-stays-f64 pattern.

Output: a fully generic `rys.rs` and `c2s.rs` with f64 byte-identity preserved, plus an f32-host Rys smoke test exercising the weight-sum identity at single precision.
</objective>

c2s.rs: the c2s coefficient table is exact rationals; KEEP the table f64, accumulate output in `F` (PATTERNS line 162). C2S_L1 is the identity matrix (Phase 9 D); CINTcommon_fac_sp s/p normalization is applied in the primitive loop, not the transform — preserve.
