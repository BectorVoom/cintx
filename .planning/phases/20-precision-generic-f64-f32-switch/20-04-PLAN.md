<objective>
Wave 2 (kernel launchers, group A): genericize the 1e, 2e, and 2c2e launchers plus the cart-to-spinor transform. The launchers keep their registered `FamilyLaunchFn` signature (a non-generic `fn(..., &mut [f64]) -> ...` — making it generic would break the function-pointer registry in `kernels/mod.rs`) and instead dispatch internally on `plan.precision` to a generic `launch_<family>_typed::<F>()`, reinterpreting the staging buffer via the Plan-01-proven bytemuck cast.

Purpose: This is the architectural crux flagged by the pattern mapper (the A5 cast / object-safety judgment call). Resolving it here for three kernels establishes the dispatch shape the remaining kernels (Plan 05) copy. The spinor transform threads `F` through its interleaved real/imag accumulation (the existing `complex_interleaved` layout, not a new `num_complex::Complex<F>` buffer).

Output: 1e/2e/2c2e launchers with a precision-dispatching outer + generic `_typed::<F>()` inner, the bytemuck staging cast wired, and the spinor transform generic over F. f64 monomorphization byte-identical (Phase 9/10/12 oracle gates unaffected).
</objective>
