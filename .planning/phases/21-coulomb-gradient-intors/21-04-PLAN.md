<objective>
Implement the `int1e_ipnuc` (hcore nuclear-attraction derivative, ∇ on the bra center summed over all nuclei) and `int1e_iprinv` (per-atom Hellmann–Feynman force, single rinv origin via the GRAD-01 env slot, no `-Z_C` factor) 3-component gradient kernels, and add byte-identity oracle parity tests vs vendored libcint 6.1.3 at atol=1e-12 on H2O/STO-3G.

Purpose: `int1e_ipnuc` is the hcore nuclear-attraction derivative every gradient needs; `int1e_iprinv` is the per-nucleus Hellmann–Feynman force term. They share the `gout_ip1` nabla applied to the nuclear-attraction Rys G-tensor and differ ONLY in (a) ipnuc loops over all nuclei accumulating `∑_C (-Z_C)·∇`, while (b) iprinv evaluates at a single origin from `plan.operator_env_params.rinv_orig` (populated by 21-01) with NO `-Z_C` charge factor (D-08). This plan depends on 21-01 (the rinv origin must exist for iprinv) and 21-02 (the manifest must register the symbols).

Output: `ipnuc`/`iprinv` branches in the 1e dispatcher; `vendor_int1e_ipnuc_{cart,sph}` + `vendor_int1e_iprinv_{cart,sph}` FFI wrappers; `one_electron_nuc_grad_parity.rs` with 4 byte-identity tests (ipnuc cart/sph, iprinv cart/sph — the iprinv tests sweep the rinv origin over each nucleus).
</objective>
