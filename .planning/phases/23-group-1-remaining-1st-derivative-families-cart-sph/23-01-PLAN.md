1. Promote `nabla1j_2e` (f12.rs:641) and `nabla1k_2e` (f12.rs:688) from private `fn` to
   `pub(crate) fn` so sibling launchers can call them (E0603 today).

   cintx maps the real 3c2e auxiliary `k` into the 2e `ll` slot (int3c2e_ip2, Pitfall 2).
   The in-tree structural reference is `nabla1l_breit` (breit.rs:1206).

   hardcodes `nabla1i_2e` at its internal nabla call. Generalize it (pass which-nabla fn +
   exponent) OR add `gout_ipk`/`gout_ipl` siblings that reuse the IDENTICAL s[0..2] mixing
   body and only swap the `nabla1*_2e(&mut g1, g, ...)` call + exponent.

is currently bra-i-only and partly private. Without this plumbing, cluster A cannot compile.
